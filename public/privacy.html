Privacy policy

A little clarity about your privacy.

Updated 18 November 2026 · Promotional website

01

What this policy covers

This policy describes the UniBuddy promotional website. The mobile application is not available through this website, and this policy does not describe the future app’s account, chat, location, or resource-sharing services. App-specific privacy information will be published before release.

02

Information on this website

You can explore this website without creating an account. The current website does not collect email addresses directly, use advertising trackers, or run analytics. Your hosting and network providers may process technical request information, including IP addresses and browser information, to deliver and secure the website.

03

Fonts and external services

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives the requests needed to deliver those font files. If those requests are unavailable, your browser uses fallback fonts. Images and website scripts are served from this website.

04

Launch notifications

When launch subscriptions are enabled, the signup button opens a separate Brevo-hosted form. An email address is submitted only if you enter it on that form and submit it. Review its operator details, privacy notice, consent choices, and unsubscribe information before subscribing. The notification list is not open while the website displays “coming soon.”

05

Cookies and preferences

The current website does not set application cookies or use browser storage for marketing. The motion preference control affects the current page session only. Your browser or infrastructure providers may have their own technical behavior and policies.

06

Your choices

You can browse without joining the launch list, disable JavaScript, block external font requests, and reduce or pause motion. If you subscribe through Brevo, use the unsubscribe link in the emails or the contact information provided on the subscription form.

07

Updates and contact

This page will be updated as the service develops. Operator contact information and subscription-specific retention details must be supplied on the live signup form before subscriptions are enabled. This website currently has no account or personal-data request portal.
