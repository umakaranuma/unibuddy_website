CONCEPT PREVIEW

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Curious?
We like that.

What is UniBuddy?+

UniBuddy is a mobile application being built for student life in Sri Lanka. It brings student connections, shared academic resources, campus spots, and focused study into one place.

Who is it for?+

Students at universities and higher education institutes across Sri Lanka. You’ll be able to find your own campus community and discover students beyond it.

Can I download the app yet?+

Not yet. We’re preparing for launch. Official download links will appear here when the app is available; we haven’t announced a release date.

Are these real app screenshots?+

The artwork on this website is concept and promotional imagery. It introduces the idea behind UniBuddy; features and visuals may change before launch.

Is UniBuddy affiliated with a university?+

UniBuddy is an independent student community project. University names in the concept artwork describe the intended community and do not imply endorsement or partnership.
